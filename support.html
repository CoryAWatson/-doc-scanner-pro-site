Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a document?

Tap the blue camera button on the home screen. Point your camera at a document — the app will auto-detect and highlight it. Tap the shutter button to capture. You can adjust and crop the result before saving.

Where are my documents stored?

All documents are stored locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to the internet. Your documents stay private and are removed if you uninstall the app.

How do I export or share a document?

Open a document, then tap the Share icon in the top toolbar. You can share as a JPEG image or convert and share as a PDF. You can also swipe left on any document in the folder view to access quick Share and Delete buttons.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Go to Settings (gear icon on the home screen) and tap "Restore Purchases". Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID and will be restored automatically.

My document previews disappeared after an update. What happened?

This can occasionally happen when iOS updates your app container. The app automatically migrates all file paths on launch. If previews are still missing, try force-quitting and reopening the app. Contact us if the issue persists.

How do I organize documents into folders?

When saving a document, tap the Category button to select an existing folder or create a new one. You can access your folders from the home screen grid.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. Go to iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Doc Scanner Professional and cancel anytime. You'll retain Pro access until the end of the current billing period.
