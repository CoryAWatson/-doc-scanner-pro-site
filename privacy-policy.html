Privacy Policy

Doc Scanner Professional  ·  Effective Date: May 1, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 4, 2026

TL;DR: Doc Scanner Professional stores all your documents locally on your device only. We do not collect, upload, or sell any of your personal data or scanned documents.

1. Information We Collect

Doc Scanner Professional is designed with privacy first. The app does not collect or transmit any personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • All scanned documents and images are stored exclusively on your device.
  • No document content is uploaded to any server.
  • No account creation is required to use the app.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are used.

2. Camera Access

The app requests access to your device's camera solely to scan documents. Camera data is processed on-device and is never transmitted externally. You can revoke camera access at any time in Settings > Privacy > Camera.

3. Local Storage

All scanned documents, thumbnails, and app settings are stored in your device's local application sandbox using SQLite and the device file system. This data is accessible only to the app and is removed when you uninstall the application.

4. Subscriptions & Purchases

In-app subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Purchase records are managed by Apple and are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Face ID / Biometric Authentication

If you enable the app lock feature, biometric data (Face ID or Touch ID) is handled exclusively by iOS's secure enclave. We never have access to your biometric data.

6. Third-Party Services

Doc Scanner Professional does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or social media services. The only third-party interaction is Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases.

7. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13, and is not directed at children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
